Pick Panic! Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 11, 2026

Pick Panic! is a party game operated by LetsChat Ai LLC. The app does not require an account and is designed to keep gameplay information on the devices being used to play. We do not sell personal information, serve third-party advertising, or track users across apps or websites.

Information You Enter

Players may enter display names and create custom lists for use during games. This information is stored locally on the device and is used only to provide the app's gameplay and customization features. LetsChat Ai LLC does not receive or store this information on its servers.

Nearby Multiplayer

If you choose to use a nearby multiplayer feature, Pick Panic! uses Apple's local-network technology to connect participating devices. Information needed for that game session, such as player names, selections, votes, and results, may be sent directly between the participating nearby devices. This session information is not sent to or stored on servers operated by LetsChat Ai LLC.

In-App Purchases

Pick Panic! offers optional non-consumable in-app purchases. Payments and purchase restoration are processed by Apple through the App Store. LetsChat Ai LLC does not receive or store your payment-card number, billing address, or Apple Account password. Apple handles purchase information according to Apple's Privacy Policy .

Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

Pick Panic! does not use third-party advertising SDKs, third-party analytics SDKs, or cross-app tracking. The app does not sell or rent personal information.

Device Permissions

Nearby multiplayer may cause iOS or iPadOS to request Local Network permission. This permission is used only to discover and communicate with nearby devices participating in a Pick Panic! game. The app does not request access to your precise location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone for its normal gameplay features.

Data Retention and Deletion

Custom lists and other saved preferences remain on the device until you remove them within the app, clear the app's data, or delete the app. Nearby game-session information is used for the active session and is not retained by LetsChat Ai LLC. Because the app does not create user accounts or store this information on our servers, there is no server-side account or gameplay record for us to delete.

Children's Privacy

Pick Panic! is a general-audience party game and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information directly to LetsChat Ai LLC, contact us so we can review the concern.

Security

We use reasonable measures intended to keep the app safe and reliable. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the app's features or privacy practices change. The updated policy will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
